# Commercial licensing proposal for 2.0

**Draft for owner and legal review; this document grants no new license and does
not replace [LICENSE](../LICENSE) or the notices in individual files.** The 2.0
release retains the existing GPL distribution terms. Commercial use
under those existing terms is not prohibited by this proposal.

## Intended offering

The requested future offering is source available, free for noncommercial use,
with a separate paid license for commercial use. A restriction on commercial use
does not meet the [Open Source Definition, section 6](https://opensource.org/osd).
Describe that offering as **source available**, rather than open source.

An alternative is to keep the GPL edition open source and charge for hosting,
support, integration, training, or a separately licensed edition where the
necessary copyright permissions exist. The GNU project's
[licensing FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html) explains that a
copyright holder can offer the same code under multiple licenses. Existing GPL
recipients retain their granted rights; changing future terms does not withdraw
past grants or third-party licenses.

## Proposed noncommercial terms to finalize

| Item | Proposed position |
| --- | --- |
| Licensor | **To be supplied by the owner**: legal name and public contact. |
| Scope | First-party code and releases explicitly identified in the final agreement. Third-party code, assets, and data retain their own terms. |
| Free use | Personal learning, experimentation, and qualifying noncommercial education/research; permit studying and modifying source. |
| Commercial use | Require a paid agreement for use supporting a business, paid services, hosted customer access, or product integration. Clarify evaluation, nonprofit, government, and sponsored research cases before adoption. |
| Redistribution | Specify permitted source/binary redistribution, required notices, modification disclosure, and treatment of derivatives. These rules are not yet settled. |
| Commercial evaluation | Decide the duration, allowed users, deployment scope, and transition to a paid agreement; no trial limit is implemented in this release. |
| Warranty and liability | Have counsel draft applicable warranty exclusions, liability limits, termination/cure provisions, governing law, and mandatory consumer-rights exceptions. |

## Paid offering to finalize

Use a written order form to identify the customer, licensed version, deployment
scope (internal use, hosted service, or OEM distribution), fees, renewal terms,
upgrade rights, and any support response commitments. Price and support terms
are owner decisions; none are promised by the application or this repository.
Provide a public sales contact and a purchase process before marketing a paid
license. License keys, payment collection, telemetry, and usage enforcement are
not implemented here.

## Conditions before adopting different terms

1. Confirm ownership or relicensing permission for **every** included first-party
   contribution. An author name or repository ownership alone is not proof.
2. Review existing file notices and the root GPL text, including their historical
   version differences. Preserve existing grants and attribution.
3. Inventory third-party code, icons, screenshots, and datasets; obtain rights
   appropriate to the intended paid distribution. Review [dependency notices](third-party-notices.md).
4. Supply the legal licensor name/contact and obtain legal review of the actual
   license and order form. Then apply consistent notices only to eligible files
   and publish a clearly identified new licensed release.

The 2.0 release uses the existing license. The proposed noncommercial-only terms
remain unadopted until those conditions are resolved.
